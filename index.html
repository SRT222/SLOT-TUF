Pack a Punch

Fun money only. You start with 500 TUF.

SPIN THE WHEEL

Pick a bet with the up and down keys (1, 2, 5 or 10 TUF), then press START. The row between the two pointers is your result: it pays its multiplier times your bet. A ×0 loses the bet and a ×1 gives it back.

THE CONTROLS

  • START or Space: spin
  • Up / Down keys: change your bet
  • Present key or ←: buy a mystery box with 5 free spins
  • Blue arrow key or →: auto spin 10, 50 or 100 times
  • TURBO (under the spin counter): spins 1.5x faster
  • reset (top left): back to 500 TUF
  • ? and the speaker (bottom left): this guide and sound on or off

Your balance is saved in this browser.

MYSTERY BOX ON THE WHEEL

About 1 spin in 101 lands on the wooden chest. The machine shakes, the marquee lights up and the box rolls a multiplier (2x, 10x, 50x or 1,000x) and 1 to 5 free spins. The free spins play automatically, each paying the wheel result times the multiplier. Your bet is locked until they finish.

BUY A MYSTERY BOX

Press the present and pick a bet. A box costs 50 times that bet and always gives 5 free spins at a rolled multiplier of 1x to 10x. The five spins together pay between 5x and 2,500x your bet:

  • 60%: 5x to 50x
  • 30%: 50x to 100x
  • about 10%: 100x to 500x
  • 1 in 5,000: the 2,500x max win

PERK BOTTLES

Every 6 to 31 spins a bottle drops down the screen. Tap it before it falls off the bottom and your next spin pays extra, at the bet you had when you caught it. Its color tells you how much:

2x   5x   10x   50x

Catching a bottle during auto spin pauses it. Press START to carry on.
